A few questions, answered.

Is this suitable if I’m completely new?

Yes. This is an introductory workshop. You’ll learn about the Records and the opening and closing practice before the guided meditation. Curiosity is enough to begin.

When is the live call?

Sunday, November 1, 2026, at 3 PM Eastern / 2 PM Central / Noon Pacific. The workshop takes place online. Register using the Square checkout link, or receive the workshop with annual Premium membership.

What if I can’t attend live?

A replay will be available, so you can return to the teaching and guided experience after the live call.

What should I bring?

A journal, something to write with, and a comfortable, quiet space where you can join the online call and settle into the meditation.

Do I have to see or hear a guide?

No. People may notice feelings, images, words, subtle impressions, or very little at first. There is no required experience and no guaranteed outcome. Treat what arises as material for personal reflection.
